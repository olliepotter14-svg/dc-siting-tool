Data sources

Every factor value in this tool links to its source. Below is the master list of the datasets behind the 13 factors and the three map overlays.

Demand
Compute demand 2027 (TWh)
IEA Electricity 2024 + national TSO long-term forecasts (BMWK, EirGrid, RTE, Energinet, Statnett, Fingrid). Cushman & Wakefield and JLL cross-checks for emerging markets.
Grid
Connection timeline (years)
Each national TSO's published 2024 connection-queue statistics — NESO TEC Register, EirGrid, Bundesnetzagentur, TenneT, RTE, Terna, REE, Svenska kraftnät, PSE, etc.
Power production per capita (kWh/yr)
Our World in Data (Ember 'Yearly Electricity Data') — fetched live by fetch_ember.py.
Forecast grid investment (USD bn)
National TSO 10-year network development plans (BMWK Stromnetz 2030, RIIO-T3, RTE Schéma, Terna PdS, etc.).
Fibre
Bandwidth (Gbps)
Ookla Speedtest Global Index (Q1 2024 median fixed-broadband download).
2030 forecast (Gbps)
National gigabit strategies (BMDV, Plan France Très Haut Débit, EU Gigabit Society 2030) + Analysys Mason.
Differentiators
Construction cost (USD/MW)
Cushman & Wakefield Global DC Construction Cost Guide 2024 + Turner & Townsend Data Centre Cost Index 2024.
Power cost (USD/kWh)
Eurostat NRG_PC_205 (EU industrial Band IE) + each national regulator's published industrial tariff (Ofgem, DEWA, SEC, etc.).
R&D techs / million + Tertiary graduate %
World Bank Indicators API (SP.POP.TECH.RD.P6, SE.TER.CUAT.BA.ZS) — fetched live by fetch_world_bank.py.
Grid carbon intensity (gCO₂/kWh)
Our World in Data (Ember) — fetched live by fetch_ember.py.
Market maturity
Live + planned DC capacity (MW)
Cushman & Wakefield Global DC Market 2024 + JLL EMEA Data Centres Q4 2024 + datacentermap.com for smaller markets.
Map overlays
IXPs + DC facilities
PeeringDB — fetched live by fetch_overlays.py.
HV transmission grid (≥380 kV)
OpenStreetMap via Overpass API.
All values carry {value, year, source, url}. To refresh: python3 scripts/fetch_ember.py, python3 scripts/fetch_world_bank.py, python3 scripts/fetch_overlays.py, then python3 scripts/build_markets_dataset.py.
